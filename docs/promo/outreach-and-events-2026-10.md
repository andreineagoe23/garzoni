## 1. App Store In-App Event (monthly, en-GB + ro)

Create in App Store Connect → your app → In-App Events. Events show in search results and on the
product page, so they're free visibility. They need a deep link into the app (or open the app's
home) and a 1920×1080 card image plus a 3:4 (1080×1440) image.

| Field | Limit | en-GB | ro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Event name | 30 | `7-Day Money Reset` | `Resetarea banilor în 7 zile` |
| Short description | 50 | `One 5-minute money lesson a day for a week.` | `O lecție de 5 minute pe zi, o săptămână.` |
| Long description | 120 | `Build a budget, start an emergency fund and keep a 7-day streak. Free, five minutes a day.` | `Fă-ți un buget, începe un fond de urgență și ține o serie de 7 zile. Gratuit, cinci minute pe zi.` |
| Badge | — | Challenge | Challenge |
| Purpose | — | Attract new users + re-engage | same |
| Dates | — | 1st to 7th of the month, then repeat with a new theme | same |

Next themes, one per month: `Investing From Zero`, `Beat Your Overdraft`, `Your First ISA`
(UK only — not in ro), `Understand Your Payslip`.

Play equivalent: Play Console → Grow → Promotional content (LiveOps) with the same copy.

students UK". Find the adviser's name on the uni's student money page; send one short email.

**Subject:** A free money-skills app for your students' resources page

> Hi [name],
>
> I'm Andrei Neagoe, a Queen Mary Computer Science graduate. I built Garzoni, a free app that
> teaches personal finance in five-minute lessons — budgeting, saving, credit, payslips and
